# Code of conduct

Abax is maintained by one person in their spare time, for people who want to learn the soroban. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to be considerate, patient and honest.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most friction is a misunderstanding.
- Keep it about the project. No harassment, no personal attacks, no discrimination of any kind.
- Give credit, cite sources, and say when you are not sure.

Reports of unacceptable behaviour can be sent privately to the maintainer through the e-mail on the GitHub profile of the repository owner. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit, remove or lock contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, shortened for a one-maintainer project.
