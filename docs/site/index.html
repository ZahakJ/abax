Learn the soroban move by move, then carry it in your head.

Abax is a soroban (Japanese abacus) trainer. It shows every bead move with the finger that makes it, drills each rule until it is quick, and then takes the beads away.

Download Read the manual

Open source, free, offline, no account. Linux, Windows, macOS and Android.

How it teaches

A scheduler tracks each skill: how likely you are to know it, how accurate and how fast you are lately, and how hard the current step is for you. Each session is a short warm-up, a focus drill on your weakest skill and a cool-down. Progress is shown as momentum over 14 days, not as a streak that breaks. How the scheduler works.

The curriculum at a glance

Fifteen skills. Each unlocks when the ones before it are known well enough.

  1. Beads
    • S0 Read and set numbers
    • S1 Add directly
    • S2 Subtract directly
  2. Small friends
    • S3 Add through 5
    • S4 Subtract through 5
  3. Big friends
    • S5 Add through 10
    • S6 Subtract through 10
    • S7 Both at once
  4. Longer numbers
    • S8 Multi-digit addition
    • S9 Mixed chains
  5. Multiply and divide
    • S10 Multiplication
    • S11 Division
  6. In your head
    • A1 Anzan, fading beads
    • A2 Flash anzan, 1 digit
    • A3 Flash anzan, 2 and 3 digits

Each skill, its rule and its examples

Three finishes

Drawn on the GPU: each bead is lit as a solid, with a highlight, a soft edge and a contact shadow. Light and dark themes for each.
